Smart Document Scanner
Photos of documents are often tilted, have shadows,
reflections or noise and do not allow direct copying
of the text. Manual data entry is slow and prone to
errors. This project proposes a desktop application
that automatically detects and corrects the document
in a photo, enhances the image and extracts the text
into an editable file.

Input: photos of printed documents, preferably a
single visible page.
Output: scanned image and recognized text, editable
and exportable as UTF-8 .txt file.
